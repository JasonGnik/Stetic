Common questions

  • Managing your subscription. Open the iPhone Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Stetic to view, change, or cancel.
  • Restoring a purchase. In Stetic, go to Settings → Restore purchases, or re-tap your plan on the paywall.
  • Deleting your account. In Stetic, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and data.
  • Free trial. Cancel anytime in your App Store subscription settings before the trial ends to avoid being charged.
