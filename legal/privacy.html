Privacy Policy

Stetic · Effective 26 June 2026

Stetic is built around a simple promise: your photos are never stored. A physique or meal photo is analyzed in the moment and then discarded — we keep only the numbers it produced (a score, a macro estimate), never the image.

This policy explains what Stetic ("we", "us", "the app") collects, why, and your choices. It applies to the Stetic iOS app and related services. By using Stetic you agree to this policy.

1. Information you provide

  • Account. When you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we receive an account identifier and, depending on the method, your email address and name. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email.
  • Profile & onboarding answers. Sex, age, height, weight, goal weight, goal, training experience, available equipment, activity level, and the obstacles/preferences you select. This is used to generate and personalize your plan.
  • Logs you create. Workouts, sets, weights and reps; meals and nutrition entries; bodyweight entries; and daily readiness check-ins (mood, confidence, readiness). These power your streak, progress, and targets.

2. Photos — analyzed, not stored

When you scan a physique photo or a meal, the image is sent to our AI provider (Google — Gemini API) for analysis and to Stetic's backend only to broker that request. We do not save your photos to our servers or database. Only the derived results are kept (for a physique scan: your score and body-composition numbers; for a meal: the detected foods and macro estimates). Google processes images on Stetic's behalf under its API terms and, on the paid API tier we use, does not use your content to train its models.

3. Health & fitness data (Apple Health)

If you grant permission, Stetic reads step count and bodyweight from Apple Health to show your activity and progress, and can write the bodyweight you log back to Apple Health. This is optional, gated behind an explicit tap, and revocable at any time in iOS Settings → Health. Health data is used only to provide app features. We never use Health data for advertising and never sell or share it.

4. Subscriptions & purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. We receive your subscription status and an anonymized purchase identifier so we can unlock Pro features. We never receive your full card number or payment credentials — Apple handles billing.

5. How we use information

  • To generate your plan, score, targets, and progress.
  • To operate core features (logging, streaks, reminders, food search).
  • To deliver local notifications you enable (training and meal reminders are scheduled on your device).
  • To maintain and secure your account and prevent abuse of our AI features.
  • To produce anonymized, aggregate statistics (e.g. trends across users). These can never identify you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the app, under their terms:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting.
  • Google (Gemini API) — AI analysis of scans, meals, and plan generation.
  • RevenueCat and Apple — subscription processing.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts — food and barcode lookups (your search text is sent to retrieve results; these are public nutrition databases).

7. Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and logs until you ask us to delete them. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at jasonkingprod@gmail.com; we will delete your data within 30 days, except where we must retain limited records to meet legal or accounting obligations. Because photos are never stored, there is nothing to delete on that front.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your records is restricted to your authenticated account through row-level security, and AI features require an authenticated session. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

Stetic is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise them, contact us at jasonkingprod@gmail.com. We do not sell personal information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will revise the effective date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: jasonkingprod@gmail.com.
