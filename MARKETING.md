# Marketing Ideas

Running list of marketing ideas for Stetic. Add freely — no idea too rough.

> **Strategy distilled from 3 teardowns (2026-06-25):** PuffCount onboarding ($40k/mo),
> Joseph Choy / Superwall (viral consumer apps via TikTok), Evan Yadagari / Locked ($14k/mo).
> The throughline: **Stetic is the Umax cult-movement play for the physique** — scan → score →
> "your potential" → tips. Lean all the way into the *looksmaxxing / "ascend"* movement; the cult
> is the moat. Sequence is fixed: **retain → prove a free format → then pay creators to scale it.**

## The launch sequence (do NOT skip ahead)

1. **Prove retention first.** No leaky bucket. Watch D1/D7, re-scan rate, trial→paid before any spend.
   (Both Joseph and Evan: scaling a non-retentive app = paying for an e-commerce one-time sale.)
2. **Post faceless content yourself** (free). Slideshows + screen-recordings of the scan/reveal.
   Cold-start flops are normal — post 5–10 before judging. Evan's first $2k/mo (Problem Pal) came
   from ONE faceless video he posted himself, before any paid creators.
3. **Find the format that pops** + confirm it converts.
4. **THEN run the influencer DM machine** to pour fuel on the proven format.
   → Influencers are the *scaling* lever, not the cold-start. Don't pay creators to test formats.

## Economics (the numbers that gate creator spend)

- **RPM = revenue per 1,000 views.** Chain: `1,000 views → % download → ~10% subscribe → × rev/sub`.
- Evan runs **RPM ~$3–4** on $30/yr · $7/wk. **Our prices are ~2×**, so our **RPM ≈ $6–8.**
- **Rule: keep CPM (paid per 1,000 views) < RPM** → every video is profitable.
- **Our higher price = higher RPM = we can outbid competitors for the same creators.** Price is a weapon.
- **Pricing decision (locked):** keep weekly **$9.99** (anchors the $59.99 annual = "SAVE 88%", and
  lifts RPM). Do NOT lower the weekly. The lever to tune is the *annual* (trial length, exit $30/yr),
  not the weekly. Only revisit weekly if data shows most buyers pick weekly + churn fast → then raise/hide it.

## Content / Organic

- **The gotcha format (our strongest):** "I put [celeb] in the app and it said…" — the rank reveal IS
  the hook. "AI ranked David Laid → Greek God 9.5." Engineer controversy: "AI rated Ronnie Coleman
  and it started a fight in the comments" (keep ONE spicy public take — the mass-monster cap — purely
  for reach; real user scoring stays honest. See [[stetic-scoring-approach]]).
- **Scan-overlay as a creator utility (free-marketing cheat code):** polish the scan reticle + score
  reveal + rank-ladder climb into a screen-record-ready visual gimmick (cf. KO's skeleton-wireframe
  wow factor). If the app makes a creator's before/after video look cool, they'll use it *for free*.
- **Slideshows / carousels** (TikTok Photo Mode, low effort, infinite scale, AI images fine): POV +
  **strong negative hooks** — "POV: the AI rated your physique a 4.2", "Our gym bro HATED what the AI
  said about his 'huge' arms". Lower-converting than talking-head but massive scale / low barrier.
- **Video (screen-recording the scan/result)** for the demo/conversion side. Repurpose one concept
  into both a slideshow and a video.
- **One face/format per account.** When a format pops, spin up a NEW brand account for it so the
  algorithm knows who to serve it to. Don't dilute one account with five formats.
- **Mine what works** before guessing: TikTok Creative Center keyword insights, Sensor Tower,
  Spy Talk — find outlier videos in the fitness-scan niche and remix the hooks.
- **Fitness influencer edits** — drop our app into edits of famous fitness influencers (UGC "app in
  the wild"). Open Qs: rights/likeness (parody-style or our own talent mimicking the format?).
- **Comment-CTA for distribution** (no bio link under 1k followers): plant "wait what app is this??"
  → reply with the name + pin it. IG kills link attribution; this comment IS the CTA.

## Partnerships / Influencer — the DM machine (Evan's playbook)

- **Channel verdict:** Evan tested Reddit / X / influencer / paid ads → **influencer won decisively.**
- **Niches to DM:** gym · self-improvement · **day-in-the-life** (his best) · looksmaxxing/aesthetic/glow-up.
  Day-in-the-life is the natural plug — "I scanned my physique, AI gave me a 6.4, here are my weak points."
- **Pick by ENGAGEMENT, not followers:** 5k–millions views/video, real comments (questions, replies —
  not emoji spam). Followers are a vanity metric.
- **It's a volume operation:** he DM'd 5,000–10,000 creators, 500/day at peak, ~100/day per account
  (rate limit) → multiple **verified** accounts w/ 1k+ followers. Response rate ~3–4% IG, ~2% TikTok.
- **DM script:** lead with **"promo"** (survives the inbox scroll) → "Hey, I'm the founder of Stetic.
  We're partnering with a select group of [gym/aesthetic] creators. Down to hop on a call?" →
  **always push to a call; never negotiate in DMs.**
- **Deal structures** (Evan's favorite = #3):
  1. Standard — $500/video or $1,200/4 (only for proven big-view creators).
  2. CPM — $1–2 per 1,000 views, **capped** (keep CPM < our RPM ~$6–8).
  3. **Min-view clause** — ~$800 with a 400–800k min-view clause; they keep posting until views
     collectively hit it. Protects against a single flop; anchors creator on full payout.
  4. Bonus — $250 + extra $250 if it hits 250k. Mixable with the others.
- **Video requirements:** app on screen **within first 15s** (before = looks like an ad & dies;
  after = retention drops). Plant + pin the "what app is this?" comment. Give creators freedom;
  approve before posting. Attribute by **revenue spikes** (no IG link tracking).
- **Scale winners:** profitable creator → renew, then renegotiate a bigger deal.

## Positioning / Strategy

- **Sell the movement, not the feature.** Umax sold *looksmaxxing*, not "a face scanner." Stetic sells
  **physique-maxxing / ascending** as an identity; "Greek god, not Olympia gut" is the manifesto.
  Cult-like is the goal.
- **High-intent adjacency:** deadline cohorts — "get aesthetic for [wedding/event], 12 weeks" — natural
  urgency for the paywall.

## Paid
