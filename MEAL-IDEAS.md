# Meal ideas — expansion (to discuss, post-onboarding)

> Parked here from the onboarding redesign convo (2026-06-26). Jason: nutrition "made easy" is a
> selling point, but meal ideas need more depth — users should be able to get MORE and more VARIED
> ideas, not just the fixed curated set.

## Today
- `MealIdeasView` — 16 curated high-protein meals by type (breakfast/lunch/dinner/snacks), one-tap log.
- "Ideas & cravings" sheet; craving feature (Gemini) generates a craving-fit food + macros + adjustments.

## The gap / what Jason wants
- Users will burn through 16 fixed ideas fast. Need a way to get **more / fresh / varied** ideas.
- Tie ideas to: their goal, remaining macros for the day, their tastes/restrictions, what's on hand.

## Options to explore (not decided)
- **Generated ideas (Gemini):** "give me 5 lunch ideas that fit my remaining macros / my goal" — infinite, personalized. Cheap (Flash). Mirrors the craving feature.
- **Filters/categories:** cuisine, high-protein, low-effort, budget, vegetarian, etc.
- **"More like this" / refresh** on the ideas list.
- **Saved/favorites** feeding a rotating set.
- Pull from a bigger curated library (more than 16) + the food catalog.

## Open questions
- Generated vs curated vs hybrid?
- How much does this need to be ready for launch vs after? (Likely after — 16 + cravings is enough for v1.)
- Cost/latency if generated per request.
