what you've got going for you
ChestYour most developed area, and it tracks, your whole routine is built around pressing. Genetics help too: wide clavicles, so you carry width up top.
FrameNaturally broad shoulders and a narrow waist. That's structure you can't train, and it means you'll look good with even a bit more muscle on it.
QuadsYou squat, so legs already have a real base. They'll respond fast once the effort goes up.
needs work
BackFlat, no width or thickness. This is training, not genetics, you give it maybe 2 light sets a week. Your single biggest fix: bring it up and your whole frame changes.
BicepsBarely any direct work, so they're small. Your insertions also sit a little high (that gap above the elbow), that part's genetic, but direct volume still adds the fullness.
CalvesAlmost never trained directly, plus high genetic insertions, the tough combo. Hard, direct calf work still puts size on them.
why you're not growing
Three things in your split are holding you back:
Your lats get ~2 sets a week while chest soaks up most of your effort.
You hit each muscle once a week instead of spreading it out.
You're running 16+ chest sets a week, and that much volume almost always means you're not taking them close to failure, so a lot of them are junk sets.
This is all from the split you gave us. Fewer, harder sets in the right places is the fix.
your new plan
Same gym time, retargeted, and we're not chasing volume. Fewer sets, each one taken to 1-2 reps from failure. Less junk, more effort where it counts. Run it 6 weeks, deload week 7.
